## Review and provenance

These are full excerpts from an iterative research workflow, not unedited first-pass generations. The selected plans came from Qwen cloud calls. Qwen Omni supplied listening reports, and Codex assisted in reviewing text, plans, alignment, and execution evidence. This page does not claim a human listening study, unanimous model approval, or measured 95% accuracy. RSI experience promotion is pending human review.

Narration-only controls share the final mix timeline, including inserted pauses, but exclude sound effects and added performance voices. They are listening references, not a without-RSI baseline. Event time ranges describe scheduled rendered tracks, which need not match the first perceptually audible sample.

The website is an original static implementation. Related project pages are linked for context; no third-party website code or graphics are copied.
