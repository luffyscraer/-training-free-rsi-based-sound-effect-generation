02 / THE APPROACH

Refine the plan. Keep the story.

An iterative audiobook sound-design pipeline separates what should be heard from when and how it should be rendered. RSI is implemented through reusable experience and prompt refinement, not model-weight training.

  1. 01

    Plan events

    Ground sound effects and brief vocal performances in the original text.

  2. 02

    Place & render

    Align text anchors, select or generate audio, then mix it with narration.

  3. 03

    Review & repair

    Check text coverage and listening evidence; revise the relevant plan, material, or mix.

  4. 04

    Retain experience

    Record failures and corrections as candidate experience for human review and later reuse.

↺

A feedback loop, not a one-pass claim. These selected versions include iterative repairs and Codex-assisted adjudication. Experience from this collection is pending human approval for RSI promotion.

03 / REPRODUCIBILITY & CREDITS

What this demo shows.

Qualitative examples

Ten selected complete excerpts demonstrate continuous sounds, brief events, and layered narration. English examples are translated passages. This collection is not a controlled benchmark, a measured perceptual-accuracy result, or a with/without-RSI experiment.

Cloud listening reports were reviewed alongside source text, plans, alignment, and execution evidence. They did not unanimously mark these mixes PASS; remaining limitations are disclosed with each sample. No direct human listening study is claimed.

Audio & implementation

These runs used Qwen cloud planning and Qwen Omni listening evaluation, IndexTTS2 narration, and retrieved/generated effects. Browser players use MP3 listening copies; WAV downloads preserve the selected mixes. Event time ranges come from rendered track placement, not measured perceptual onsets.
